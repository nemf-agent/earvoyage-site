Delete your EarVoyage account

In the app

Open EarVoyage, go to Profile, then Delete account. You'll be asked to sign in again to confirm. Your account is deactivated at once and deleted after 30 days.

Without the app

Email support@earvoyage.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profile, listening history, library, coins, bonus coins, and unlocked episodes. Purchase records are kept as required for accounting, without your name or email.

VIP subscribers

Deleting your account does not cancel VIP billing. Cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
