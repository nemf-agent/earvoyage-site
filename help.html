Signed up with Apple on iPhone? On Android, use Continue with email with your Apple ID email (or your Hide My Email address).

How do coins work?

Coins unlock episodes of any series. Bonus coins are used first. Bonus coins that come with a coin pack never expire; other bonus coins expire after 30 days. Purchased coins never expire.

What is auto-unlock?

When it is on for a series, the next locked episode unlocks with your coins as you listen. Turn it off in the player or under Profile, Manage auto-unlock.

How do I cancel VIP?

Profile, Manage VIP, Cancel VIP, then confirm in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.

How do I restore purchases?

Open Store, About coins, then Restore purchases. It is also on the VIP plans screen and in Profile.

My VIP is missing on a new phone.

Sign in to the account you subscribed with. VIP stays with the account and the store you bought it from. Still missing? Email support@earvoyage.com.

How do refunds work?

Apple and Google handle refunds under their policies. For an App Store purchase, ask at reportaproblem.apple.com. For a Google Play purchase, ask from your order history in Google Play.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile, Delete account. The steps and what is deleted are at earvoyage.com/delete-account.
