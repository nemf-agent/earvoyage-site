How do coins work?

Coins unlock episodes of any series. Bonus coins are used first and expire after 30 days; purchased coins never expire.

What is auto-unlock?

When it is on for a series, the next locked episode unlocks with your coins as you listen. Turn it off in the player or under Profile, Manage auto-unlock.

How do I cancel VIP?

Profile, Manage VIP, Cancel VIP, then confirm in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
